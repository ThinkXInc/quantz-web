Diverse Plans to Suit Every User

  • Light Plan

    Designed for the occasional user, the Light Plan offers an economical solution to mobile connectivity. For a monthly fee of $25 (excluding tax), subscribers receive a bundle of 30 minutes of talk time. This plan is ideal for those who prioritize texting and light calling over data usage. Additional usage rates apply at 26 cents per 30-second increment.

  • Basic Plan

    Tailored for moderate users, the Basic Plan provides an extended talk time of 60 minutes for a monthly charge of $35 (excluding tax). This plan suits subscribers who enjoy keeping in touch via calls without the need for extensive data services. Beyond the included minutes, calls are billed at 26 cents per 30-second segment.

  • Plus Plan

    The Plus Plan is perfect for users seeking more from their mobile experience. At $45 per month (excluding tax), this plan includes 90 minutes of call time and rewards subscribers with 500MB of bonus data for on-the-go internet access. Calls beyond the allotted minutes are priced at 24 cents per 30 seconds, providing a balance between affordability and utility.

  • Premium Plan

    For the most demanding mobile users, the Premium Plan offers a comprehensive package. Priced at $55 monthly (excluding tax), customers enjoy 120 minutes of talk time along with 1GB of data, ideal for browsing, streaming, and staying connected. Additional talk time is charged at a reduced rate of 22 cents per 30 seconds, making this plan a leading choice for heavy users who require both extensive talk time and data access.

Each of these plans is designed to cater to different user needs, from basic calling facilities to more robust packages combining talk time with data perks. Subscribers can choose a plan that best fits their lifestyle and usage patterns, ensuring they always have the right amount of talk time and data at their fingertips. Moreover, all plan holders can take advantage of our various add-ons and features, such as data top-ups, international calling packages, and exclusive member discounts, further enhancing their mobile experience with 6thMobile.
